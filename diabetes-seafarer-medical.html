Diabetes and Ship Medical Fitness

Diabetes is increasingly common among maritime candidates. A diagnosis alone does not automatically end eligibility. Operational stability and risk management are key considerations.

The main concern is not the label “diabetes” — it is risk of sudden instability during long voyages.

What doctors assess

  • Stability of blood sugar control
  • Medication safety onboard
  • History of hypoglycemia
  • Associated complications

Temporary Unfit

Many borderline cases are given temporary observation period rather than permanent rejection. Demonstrated control improves eligibility.
