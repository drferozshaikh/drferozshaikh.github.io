Declared Unfit in Seafarer Medical? What To Do

If you have been declared “unfit” or “temporarily unfit” in your seafarer medical exam, don’t panic.

Common Reasons for Medical Unfit

  • High blood pressure
  • Diabetes
  • Eyesight problems
  • Heart / ECG abnormalities

What You Should Do Next

  1. Understand the exact reason for rejection
  2. Correct the medical issue
  3. Get proper guidance before reapplying
