Basic Self-Checks Before Booking PEME

Many candidates prefer to review these basic parameters before travelling for medical:

• Recent blood pressure reading
• Fasting blood sugar or HbA1c (if history or family history)
• Liver function test (if alcohol use or fatty liver history)
• BMI (weight vs height)
• ECG if age above 35 or cardiac symptoms
• Stability after recent surgery


These are not mandatory for this tool, but awareness reduces unexpected delays.

Risk Awareness Questions

1. Blood Pressure
During any previous medical you were told “BP thoda high hai”
You are taking BP tablet or recently started medication
BP was repeated multiple times during past medical
I have not checked my BP recently
2. Blood Sugar / Diabetes
Fasting sugar or HbA1c was borderline or high in past reports
You are on diabetes medication or insulin
Doctor advised better sugar control before fitness clearance
I have not checked sugar recently
3. Liver Function (SGOT / SGPT / Fatty Liver)
SGOT/SGPT/GGT reported high in past reports
You were told you have fatty liver/diagnosed on ultrasound (USG)
Regular alcohol intake with abnormal reports in the past
I have not checked liver tests recently
4. Cardiac / ECG
ECG showed abnormality
You were advised TMT or cardiology review
Age above 35 with BP, sugar or cholesterol issues
5. Weight / BMI
BMI above 30 or overweight noted in past medical
Doctor advised weight reduction before joining
6. Vision / LASIK / Colour Vision
Recent LASIK or refractive surgery
Colour vision issue noted previously
7. Surgery History
Surgery within last 6 months
Ongoing recovery or implant present
8. Neurological History
History of seizures / epilepsy / akdi / fits
On anti-epileptic medication
History of unexplained blackout or loss of consciousness
9. Previous Medical outcome/Temporary Unfit
Previously declared Temporary Unfit
Same issue not fully corrected yet
10. Chronic Medication / Disclosure Concern
On long-term medication
Unsure whether to disclose certain history
