Basic Awareness Before Booking PEME

Typical parameters candidates review before medical:

• Blood pressure • Blood sugar / HbA1c • Liver function tests • BMI (weight vs height) • ECG if age above 35 • Stability after recent surgery

These are awareness indicators only.
