PEME Risk Self-Assessment Tool

Pre-Employment Medical Risk Awareness for Seafarers

This tool provides educational risk awareness only. It does NOT determine fitness certification. Final decision rests with examining authority and employer regulations.

Before Booking PEME, Consider Checking:

• Recent blood pressure readings
• Fasting blood sugar / HbA1c (if history)
• Liver function test if alcohol use or fatty liver history
• BMI calculation
• Stability after recent surgery
• Any previous temporary unfit status

Risk Assessment Questions

1. Blood Pressure Stability
BP ≥140/90 on multiple occasions
Recently started/changed BP medication
Variable readings during prior medical
2. Blood Sugar / Diabetes
Borderline or elevated HbA1c
On diabetes medication
Recent hypoglycaemia episode
3. Liver Function
Elevated SGOT/SGPT/GGT in last 6 months
Fatty liver diagnosis
Regular alcohol intake with abnormal reports
4. Cardiac Evaluation
Abnormal ECG/TMT finding
Advised cardiology review
Age >35 with risk factors
5. BMI / Weight
BMI >30
Associated fatty liver or metabolic issues
6. Vision / LASIK
Recent LASIK (<6 months)
Previous colour vision issue
7. Respiratory
Asthma requiring inhaler
Recent respiratory hospitalization
8. Surgery History
Surgery within last 6 months
Implants or ongoing recovery
9. Previous Temporary Unfit
Previously declared temporary unfit
Reason not yet fully resolved
10. Chronic Medication / Disclosure Concern
On long-term medication
Unsure about disclosure requirements
