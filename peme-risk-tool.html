Basic Self-Checks Before Booking PEME

Many candidates prefer reviewing these parameters before travelling for medical:

• Recent blood pressure reading
• Fasting blood sugar or HbA1c
• Liver function test if alcohol or fatty liver history
• BMI (weight vs height)
• ECG if age above 35
• Stability after recent surgery
