Seafarer Medical Requirements in India (DG Shipping Guidelines Explained)

Many candidates preparing for ship joining ask: “What are the medical requirements for seafarers in India?”

Seafarer medical fitness is assessed through a Pre-Employment Medical Examination (PEME) based on maritime safety standards. The purpose is to ensure that a seafarer can safely perform duties during long voyages.

Medical fitness is not about minor illness — it is about assessing the risk of sudden medical events at sea where advanced care may not be immediately available.

General Fitness Requirements

A seafarer is expected to have:
  • Stable blood pressure
  • Controlled blood sugar
  • Adequate eyesight and colour vision
  • No high-risk cardiac condition
  • Acceptable BMI range

Common Reasons for Temporary Unfit

Temporary unfit decisions often occur due to:
  • High blood pressure during exam
  • Elevated liver enzymes
  • Uncontrolled diabetes
  • Abnormal ECG findings
  • Recent illness or surgery

How to Prepare Before PEME

Basic preparation can reduce chances of temporary unfit:
  • Control BP and sugar before booking
  • Avoid alcohol before tests
  • Maintain stable weight
  • Carry previous medical records
Before booking your seafarer medical, understanding possible risk factors can help avoid unnecessary delays.
