DG Shipping Medical Mein Fail Kyu Hote Hai?

Real Cases + Practical Solutions (India Guide 2026)

INTRODUCTION

Agar koi seafarer banne ka plan kar raha hai ya already maritime line mein hai, toh ek cheez samajhni zaroori hai:

Medical is NOT just a formality.

Bohot candidates last stage par reject ya temporary unfit ho jaate hain — aur unhe samajh bhi nahi aata ki galti kaha hui.

Yeh guide real practical experience par based hai — wo cheezein jo koi agent ya institute clearly nahi batata.

DG Shipping Medical test har Indian seafarer ke liye mandatory hai. Ye test ensure karta hai ki candidate physically aur mentally fit hai sea duty ke liye.

Lekin har saal kai seafarers medical test mein fail ho jaate hain. Is guide mein real cases aur unke practical solutions discuss kiye gaye hain taaki future candidates apni preparation sahi tarike se kar sakein.

SECTION 1: BLOOD PRESSURE (BP)

Reality

Normal dikhne wale candidates bhi fail ho jaate hain.

Reason: Anxiety BP / White coat hypertension.

Kai baar candidate physically fit hota hai, par stress ke wajah se BP temporarily badh jaata hai.

⚠ Common Mistakes

  • Exam se pehle tension lena
  • Coffee / chai peena
  • Poor sleep
  • Caffeine aur stress ka high level

✅ What Works

  • 3–5 din proper sleep lo
  • Salt kam karo
  • Relaxed mindset rakho
  • Test ke din caffeine avoid karo
  • Deep breathing aur meditation helpful hai

SECTION 2: DIABETES (SUGAR)

Reality

Bohot log unknowingly diabetic hote hain.

HbA1c test fail kar deta hai.

DG Shipping medical mein sirf fasting sugar nahi, long-term sugar control bhi check hota hai.

⚠ Common Mistake

  • Sirf fasting sugar check karna
  • Last-minute crash diet ya medicine lena

✅ What Works

  • 1–2 months pehle se sugar control karo
  • Diet + walking daily
  • Doctor ke prescribed plan follow karo
  • No last-minute jugaad
  • Stress aur alcohol se door raho

SECTION 3: EYESIGHT & COLOUR VISION

Reality

Sabse bada shock yahi hota hai.

Kai candidates ko pata hi nahi hota ki unhe color vision issue hai.

⚠ Common Myths

  • “Lasik karwa ke sab thik ho jayega”
  • “Colour blindness ignore ho jayega”

✅ Truth

  • Colour vision fail = major issue
  • Lasik allowed hai, par specific conditions ke saath
  • Eye test se pehle apna check-up karao
  • Minor refractive error ke liye proper glasses use karo
  • Alternate maritime roles consider karo

SECTION 4: LIVER (VERY COMMON)

Reality

Fatty liver extremely common hai India mein.

LFT fail hone ke cases badh rahe hain.

⚠ Reasons

  • Alcohol consumption
  • Poor diet
  • No exercise
  • Junk food aur sugary drinks

✅ Fix

  • 1–3 months lifestyle correction
  • Healthy diet aur exercise
  • Alcohol completely avoid karo
  • Green vegetables & hydration

SECTION 5: OBESITY & BMI ISSUES

Reality

BMI 30+ hone par candidate fail ho sakta hai.

⚠ Common Mistakes

  • Crash diet
  • No exercise
  • Junk food

✅ What Works

  • Balanced diet
  • Regular workout
  • BMI 18.5–25 maintain karo

SECTION 6: DENTAL & HEARING

  • Dental infection ignore mat karo
  • Regular check-up zaroori hai
  • Hearing loss bhi rejection cause ho sakta hai

SECTION 7: MENTAL HEALTH & SUBSTANCE USE

  • Stress aur isolation common hai
  • Drug use = direct rejection
  • Mental fitness equally important hai

SECTION 8: REAL CASES (PRACTICAL UNDERSTANDING)

Case 1: Young Cadet – Unexpected BP Failure

Age: 23 yrs | Role: Deck Cadet
BP Reading during medical: 140/90
Result: TEMPORARY UNFIT

What actually happened?

  • Candidate was physically fit and active
  • No history of hypertension
  • But severe anxiety before test
  • Had coffee and poor sleep previous night

Why doctor declared unfit?

  • Risk of uncontrolled BP at sea
  • Single high reading cannot be ignored

What was done?

  • Advised 5–7 days proper rest
  • No caffeine
  • Repeated BP monitoring in calm condition

Final outcome:

Repeat test → BP normal → declared FIT

Learning for you:

  • Even healthy candidates can fail due to preparation mistakes
  • BP failure is often avoidable with correct guidance

Case 2: Officer – Hidden Diabetes Issue

Age: 28 yrs | Rank: Officer
Finding: HbA1c elevated
Result: DECLARED UNFIT

What actually happened?

  • Candidate checked only fasting sugar (which was borderline normal)
  • Ignored long-term sugar control
  • No symptoms → assumed everything fine

Why doctor rejected?

  • High HbA1c indicates long-term uncontrolled sugar
  • Risk of hypoglycaemia at sea (dangerous situation)

What could have been done earlier?

  • Check HbA1c 1–2 months before medical
  • Start diet + walking plan
  • Control sugar gradually

Actual recovery path:

  • 3 months structured diet
  • Weight control
  • Medical supervision

Final outcome:

Re-medical → FIT

Learning for you:

  • Last-minute preparation does NOT work
  • Early planning can prevent rejection

Case 3: Engineer – Fatty Liver Issue

Age: 30 yrs | Role: Engineer
Finding: High liver enzymes + fatty liver
Result: TEMPORARY UNFIT

What actually happened?

  • Regular alcohol consumption
  • Sedentary lifestyle
  • No prior testing done

Why unfit?

  • Liver dysfunction risk at sea
  • Need to confirm stability before clearance

What was done?

  • Strict alcohol restriction
  • Diet correction
  • Exercise routine started

Timeline:

  • 6–8 weeks improvement

Final outcome:

Repeat LFT normal → FIT

Learning for you:

  • Many conditions are reversible
  • But require planning BEFORE medical

Important Insight:

In most borderline cases, candidates do not fail because of disease — they fail because of lack of preparation and wrong timing.

This is where proper guidance can prevent unnecessary rejection.

SECTION 9: TEMPORARY UNFIT – RECOVERY

  • Reattempt after preparation

  • No alcohol

  • Light food
  • Carry all documents
