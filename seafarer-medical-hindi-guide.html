Seafarer Medical Examination (PEME) – Hindi Guide

Bahut se Indian seafarers Google par search karte hain: “shipping medical kaise hota hai?”, “merchant navy medical test kya hote hain?”, ya “BP high ho toh ship medical fail hota hai kya?”

Yeh page simple Hindi mein explain karta hai ki seafarer medical examination (PEME) mein kya check kiya jata hai aur kaun-se medical issues kabhi-kabhi temporary unfit decision ka reason ban sakte hain.

Ship par long voyage ke dauran advanced hospital facilities turant available nahi hoti. Isliye seafarer medical examination ka purpose hota hai yeh ensure karna ki candidate safely apni duties perform kar sake.

Shipping Medical Mein Kaun-Se Tests Hote Hain

Typical PEME examination mein yeh cheezein check ki jati hain:
  • Height, weight aur BMI
  • Blood pressure measurement
  • Blood tests (sugar, liver function etc.)
  • Urine examination
  • ECG ya heart evaluation
  • Eyesight aur colour vision test

Blood Pressure (BP) Concern

Bahut se candidates poochte hain:
  • Shipping medical mein BP high aaye toh kya hoga?
  • Kya high BP se medical fail ho jata hai?
Agar BP reading high aaye toh doctor usually reading repeat karte hain. Single high reading ka matlab permanent unfit nahi hota.

Diabetes / Blood Sugar

Common questions:
  • Agar sugar borderline ho toh medical pass ho sakta hai?
  • Kya diabetes wale seafarer ship join kar sakte hain?
Controlled diabetes kabhi-kabhi acceptable ho sakti hai depending on stability aur risk evaluation.

Diabetes in Seafarer Medical

Fatty Liver aur Liver Enzymes

Candidates aksar poochte hain:
  • Fatty liver hone se shipping medical reject ho jata hai kya?
  • SGPT high ho toh ship medical delay ho sakta hai?
Mild fatty liver alone usually permanent rejection ka reason nahi hota, lekin abnormal liver enzyme levels further evaluation require kar sakte hain.

Temporary Unfit Ka Matlab

Temporary unfit ka matlab permanent rejection nahi hota. Iska matlab hota hai ki candidate ko:
  • further evaluation
  • repeat test
  • ya treatment stabilization
ki zarurat ho sakti hai.
