Seafarer Medical Guidance (Hindi Explanation)

Many Indian seafarers search medical questions in Hindi such as: “shipping medical mein BP high ho toh kya hoga?” “fatty liver hone se ship medical pass hoga?”

This page explains common seafarer medical concerns in simple language so that candidates can understand possible medical findings before booking their PEME examination.

The purpose is not to replace medical examination but to provide basic awareness of how certain medical findings are interpreted during seafarer medical assessment.

Blood Pressure (BP)

Common Hindi search questions:
  • Shipping medical mein BP high aaye toh kya hota hai?
  • Merchant navy medical BP limit kya hai?
Blood pressure readings are often repeated during examination. Single high reading does not automatically mean permanent unfit.

Diabetes / Blood Sugar

Common questions:
  • Agar sugar borderline ho toh ship medical pass ho sakta hai?
  • Diabetes hone se seafarer medical reject ho jata hai kya?
Controlled diabetes may still be acceptable depending on stability and risk evaluation.

Liver Enzymes / Fatty Liver

Common questions:
  • Fatty liver hone se shipping medical fail ho jata hai kya?
  • SGPT high ho toh ship joining delay ho sakta hai?
Mild fatty liver alone may not lead to permanent unfitness but abnormal enzyme levels may require evaluation.

Eyesight and Colour Vision

Common questions:
  • Merchant navy eyesight requirement kya hai?
  • Colour blindness hone se deck officer ban sakte hain?
Certain maritime roles require stricter colour vision standards.

Temporary Unfit Meaning

Common questions:
  • Temporary unfit ka matlab kya hota hai?
  • Kya temporary unfit ke baad dobara medical ho sakta hai?
Temporary unfit usually means additional evaluation or treatment is required before final decision.
